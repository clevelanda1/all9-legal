All9 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

All9 Golf ("All9," "we," "us") built All9 as a hands-free, voice-first golf notebook. This policy explains what information the app collects, what stays only on your phone, and what leaves it and why.

The short version

Your scores, notes, handicap estimate and course history are stored on your phone. A few things leave it: who you are (your account), whether you subscribe to All9 Plus, crash reports, anonymous usage events, your location to find nearby courses and the weather, and a marker that stops one phone from claiming the free trial twice. When the AI caddie is on (All9 Plus, or your five free trial rounds) the text of your notes and the round details it needs to coach you are sent to our AI provider. If you don't have All9 Plus, a short summary of each finished round (never your notes or your words) is also sent to that provider so it can write notifications about your game. Your voice recordings never leave your phone.

Account information

All9 requires a free account to use the app, created through Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple. We receive and store:

  • Your email address
  • Your display name, if your sign-in provider shares one

We use this only to identify your account and, if you subscribe, to attach your All9 Plus subscription to it. We do not use it for marketing and do not sell it.

Location

All9 uses your device's location to identify which golf course you're standing on, to follow you from hole to hole during a round, and to measure distances. Following you around the course happens entirely on your device. To find courses near you and the current weather, the app sends your approximate coordinates to our course database provider (OpenGolfAPI) and our weather provider (Open-Meteo). Those requests carry no account information. Your location is never sent to or stored on our servers, and never sent to our AI provider.

Microphone and voice

All9 listens during your round so you can call out your score and notes without stopping to type. Speech-to-text conversion happens entirely on your device. Your raw voice audio is never recorded to a file, never transmitted anywhere, and never leaves your phone — not to us, not to any third party, regardless of whether you're a free or paying user.

Your round data

Your scores, spoken notes, course history, and handicap estimate are stored locally in a database on your device. We do not keep a copy. When the AI caddie is on, the parts it needs pass through our server on their way to our AI provider (see below) and are not stored there. When you don't have All9 Plus, the app also sends a summary of each finished round for notifications about your game (see below). There is currently no cloud backup — if you lose or replace your phone without transferring the app's data, that history is not recoverable by us or by you.

The AI caddie (All9 Plus and the free trial)

When the AI caddie is on — for All9 Plus subscribers, and during your five free trial rounds — the app sends our AI provider, Anthropic, what it needs to coach you: the transcribed text of notes you spoke or typed (never audio), your scores, your club distances, details of the hole you're playing (par, yardage, hazards, wind), and patterns from your past rounds and range sessions. It is used only to write your advice and review your rounds, and is not used to train AI models. Your GPS location, voice recordings, name and email are never sent to it.

Notifications about your game

If you don't have All9 Plus, All9 can send you a short notification or two after a round about something it noticed in your own game, for example a pattern that keeps showing up. To write them, when a round ends the app sends a summary of that round to our server, which passes it to Anthropic to put into words. The summary contains:

  • Your score against par on each hole
  • How many fairways and greens you hit
  • Short plain labels for the faults and strengths the round showed, and how often a fault has come up across your last few rounds
  • Your score against par in your last few rounds
  • How many notes you spoke, how many rounds you have played, and whether you are on the free trial

It does not contain what you said, your name, your location, the course you played, or your account identifier. It is used only to write these notifications and is not used to train AI models. We do not keep the text of the notifications on our servers; they are scheduled on your phone and delivered by your phone's own notification system, whether the app is open or closed.

To limit how often we do this, we record that a round has had its notifications written (your account identifier, the round's identifier and the time), and we keep a log of what each AI request cost, linked to your account (which feature made it, the model, how large it was and its price, but never what was sent or what came back). A small, fixed share of players are deliberately sent no notifications so that we can tell whether they are useful. These records are removed with your account (see below), apart from the cost totals, which are kept without your account identifier.

You can turn notifications off at any time in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → All9. Your rounds, notes and scores stay on your phone either way.

One free trial per phone

To keep the free trial to one per phone, we use Apple's DeviceCheck service. When your trial ends, All9 asks Apple to set a marker on your device; when a new account starts a trial, we ask Apple whether that marker is already set. Apple stores the marker. It does not tell us who you are, and it is not removed if you delete your account, which is the point. If a phone's trial has already been used, we record against the account (your account identifier) that it was not given a new trial. We do not store a device identifier.

Crash and error reporting

If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, a report is sent to our crash-reporting provider, Sentry. That report includes a stack trace, your device type and OS version, the app version, and your account id (so we can tell how many of your sessions have been affected if you report a problem). It never includes your round data, your notes, your scores, or your voice — those stay on your device as described above.

Usage analytics

All9 records anonymous usage events — for example, starting a round, using a free trial round, opening a notification, or viewing the subscription screen — to understand how the app is used and improve it. These events are linked to your account ID only, never your name or email, and never include your notes, scores, locations or course names. We use PostHog to process this data.

Subscriptions and payment

All9 Plus subscriptions are purchased and managed through the Apple App Store (or Google Play, where available). We do not receive or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Apple/Google. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status, which tells our servers only whether your subscription is currently active, not your payment information.

Service providers

We rely on the following providers to operate the parts of All9 described above:

  • Supabase — account authentication, subscription-status storage, and the records described above
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Apple / Google — sign-in and payment processing; Apple also provides DeviceCheck for the one-trial-per-phone rule
  • Anthropic — the AI caddie (for All9 Plus subscribers and free trial rounds) and the notifications about your game (round summaries, for players without All9 Plus)
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting
  • PostHog — anonymous usage analytics
  • OpenGolfAPI — course information, found using your approximate location
  • Open-Meteo — weather conditions, found using your approximate location

Only Anthropic receives your note text and round details, and only when the AI caddie is on; for notifications about your game it receives a round summary as described above, never your note text. No provider receives your voice recordings.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from within the app: Setup → Put The Bag Away → Delete account instead. This permanently removes your account, your subscription record, your trial and notification records and your sign-in from our systems. The AI cost log keeps its totals but no longer has your account identifier on it. It does not delete your local round data — that data was never tied to your account and remains on your device unless you separately reset it from Setup. Apple's DeviceCheck marker, described above, stays with the device.

Children's privacy

All9 is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data security

We use industry-standard practices to protect the account and subscription information described above. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of All9 after a change means you accept the updated policy.
