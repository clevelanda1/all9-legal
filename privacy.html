All9 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

All9 Golf ("All9," "we," "us") built All9 as a hands-free, voice-first golf notebook. This policy explains what information the app collects, what stays only on your phone, and the one specific exception to that.

The short version

Almost everything about your round — your scores, your spoken notes, your handicap estimate, your course history — is stored only on your phone and is never sent to us. The only things that ever leave your device are: who you are (your account), whether you're subscribed to All9 Plus, a crash report if the app fails, and — only if you're a Plus subscriber and only for a note you spoke that the app's coaching feature flags as worth a closer read — the text of that one note.

Account information

All9 requires a free account to use the app, created through Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple. We receive and store:

  • Your email address
  • Your display name, if your sign-in provider shares one

We use this only to identify your account and, if you subscribe, to attach your All9 Plus subscription to it. We do not use it for marketing and do not sell it.

Location

All9 uses your device's location to identify which golf course you're standing on and to follow you from hole to hole during a round. This location data is processed entirely on your device to look up the nearest course — your position is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Microphone and voice

All9 listens during your round so you can call out your score and notes without stopping to type. Speech-to-text conversion happens entirely on your device. Your raw voice audio is never recorded to a file, never transmitted anywhere, and never leaves your phone — not to us, not to any third party, regardless of whether you're a free or paying user.

Your round data

Your scores, spoken notes, course history, and handicap estimate are stored locally in a database on your device only. We do not have a copy. There is currently no cloud backup — if you lose or replace your phone without transferring the app's data, that history is not recoverable by us or by you.

The one exception: All9 Plus coaching

If you subscribe to All9 Plus, the app's AI caddie feature occasionally flags a specific note from your round as worth a deeper, more personalized response. In that case — and only that case — the transcribed text of that one note (never audio, never your full round, never a full conversation) is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate that response. Nothing else about your round accompanies it beyond what's needed to give useful advice (e.g. the hole you're on, relevant course conditions).

Crash and error reporting

If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, a report is sent to our crash-reporting provider, Sentry. That report includes a stack trace, your device type and OS version, the app version, and your account id (so we can tell how many of your sessions have been affected if you report a problem). It never includes your round data, your notes, your scores, or your voice — those stay on your device as described above.

Subscriptions and payment

All9 Plus subscriptions are purchased and managed through the Apple App Store (or Google Play, where available). We do not receive or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Apple/Google. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status, which tells our servers only whether your subscription is currently active, not your payment information.

Service providers

We rely on the following providers to operate the parts of All9 described above:

  • Supabase — account authentication and subscription-status storage
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Apple / Google — sign-in and payment processing
  • Anthropic — AI analysis of the specific flagged note text described above, for Plus subscribers only
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting

None of these providers receive your round data, your notes, or your voice, except for the single flagged note described above.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from within the app: Setup → Put The Bag Away → Delete account instead. This permanently removes your account and subscription record from our systems. It does not delete your local round data — that data was never tied to your account and remains on your device unless you separately reset it from Setup.

Children's privacy

All9 is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data security

We use industry-standard practices to protect the account and subscription information described above. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of All9 after a change means you accept the updated policy.
