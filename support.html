All9 Support

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Need help? Email us at all9golf@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

How do I cancel my All9 Plus subscription?

All9 Plus is billed and managed through the Apple App Store, not through All9 directly. On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → All9 Plus → Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.

I subscribed but the app still shows me as free. What do I do?

Open the paywall (Setup → Upgrade, or tap the caddie icon on the scorecard) and tap Restore Purchases. This re-links an existing App Store purchase to your All9 account without charging you again.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Setup → "Put The Bag Away" → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and subscription record. It does not delete your round history — that data lives only on your device and is untouched by account deletion.

How do I clear my round history from this device?

Round data is stored locally and isn't backed up. If you want to reset it, look for the reset option in Setup — be aware this cannot be undone and there is no cloud copy to restore from.

All9 isn't hearing me / voice notes aren't showing up

Check that microphone access is allowed for All9 in your phone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone. All9 also needs an active round in progress (tap Start Round) — it only listens during a live round, not in the background otherwise.

The course isn't being detected correctly

Check that Location access is allowed for All9 (Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → All9 → While Using the App, or Always if you want it to detect arrival automatically). If you're at a shared clubhouse with multiple courses, All9 may ask you to confirm which one you're playing.

The app crashed or something looks broken

Email all9golf@gmail.com with what you were doing when it happened — a crash report is sent to us automatically, and knowing the context helps us track it down faster.

I have a feature request or general feedback

We'd like to hear it — email all9golf@gmail.com.
